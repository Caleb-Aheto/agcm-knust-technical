Universal Pro Audio Engineering • Live FOH & Post-Production

Mixing, Recording, Editing & Mastering Blueprint

Universal ground-up audio engineering workflows applicable to any live console or DAW—from stage gain staging and subtractive EQ to multitrack editing and broadcast loudness mastering.

Live & Studio -18 dBFS Core

Ground-Up Mixing

Build every mix in hierarchical layers: calibrate room/PA phase first, lock in -18 dBFS preamp gain, anchor sub/bass summation, carve midrange pockets for instruments, and place Lead Vocals at 0 dB unity.

Capture 24-Bit / 48 kHz

Multitrack Recording

Tap pre-EQ/pre-fader stems with 12–18 dB of transient headroom so zero drum hits clip at 0.00 dBFS. Verify phase coherence across multi-mic drum kits and dual-mic sources before hitting record.

Surgical Prep Clip Gain + De-Bleed

Post-Production Editing

Clean raw stems before touching plugins: time-align lagging room/tom mics, apply strip-silence with smooth crossfades on idle backing vocal tracks, and level erratic phrases with manual clip gain.

Final Polish -1.0 dBTP Ceiling

Mastering & Delivery

Apply linear-phase sub cleanup (HPF 28–32 Hz), gentle 1.5–2 dB bus glue compression, mid/side stereo enhancement above 250 Hz, and True-Peak brickwall limiting calibrated for streaming platforms.

Stage Mic Color-Coding SOP & Gear Health Tracker

Standardized vocal mic color-coding, 5-second console soft-swap recovery, and live equipment health tracking for every AGCM-KNUST service.

Vocal Microphone Color-Tape & Capsule Standard

Every vocal mic on stage carries a matching colored electrical tape band on the handle, matching XLR boot ring, and matching 32SX Scribble Strip color.

RED TAPE Ch 13 • In 13

Lead Vocal (LV)

Primary authentic capsule (SM58 / Beta 58A). Center stage stand. Reserved strictly for Lead.

BLUE TAPE Ch 14 • In 14

BV 1 (Soprano)

Matched cardioid dynamic. Stage-left front position. HPF at 135 Hz + Expander at -36 dB.

GREEN TAPE Ch 15 • In 15

BV 2 (Alto)

Matched cardioid dynamic. 2nd position from stage-left. Assigned to BV DCA Group.

YELLOW TAPE Ch 16 • In 16

BV 3 (Tenor)

Add +6 dB preamp boost over BV1 to balance Tenor output + -5.5 dB cut at 700 Hz.

PURPLE TAPE Ch 17 • In 17

BV 4 (Aux Vocal)

Keep strictly on far BV stand; if LV grabs Purple by mistake, execute Soft-Swap below.

Emergency Mid-Service Recovery (If Singers Swap Mics Live)

Console Soft-Swap (5 Seconds)

Swap 32SX Digital Input Sources

Tap HOME → Input → Digital Patching on the 32SX. Route Channel 13 (LV) to read Analog Input 17, and route Channel 17 (BV4) to Analog Input 13.

Capsule Compensation EQ

Apply Vocal Rescue Curve

Lower HPF to 80 Hz, boost +4.5 dB @ 240 Hz (warmth), cut -4.5 dB @ 786 Hz (nasal honk), and add +3.5 dB shelf @ 8 kHz.

Monitor & DCA Check

Verify Wedge & Capture Routing

Ensure the swapped channel is pulled out of the BV Mute/DCA group so the Lead Vocalist is never muted between songs.
