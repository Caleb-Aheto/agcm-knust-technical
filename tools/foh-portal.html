Live FOH Mixing & Broadcast Mastering Blueprint

Hierarchical live Front-of-House mixing, subtractive EQ, bus subgrouping, and True-Peak broadcast loudness mastering.

1. Gain & Phase -18 dBFS Core

Ground-Up FOH Mixing

Build every mix in hierarchical layers: verify PA & subwoofer crossover phase first, lock in -18 dBFS digital preamp gain (-6 dBFS peak headroom), anchor Kick and Bass low-end summation, carve midrange pockets for instruments, and place Lead Vocals at 0 dB unity.

2. Spectral Carving High-Pass + Notch

Subtractive EQ & Dynamic Control

Engage High-Pass Filters on all non-sub sources (80–135 Hz on vocals, 350 Hz on overheads), use key-filtered gates on drums (145 Hz Kick, 95 Hz Floor Tom), and apply 2:1–3:1 soft expanders on vocals before adding gentle 3–4 dB compression.

3. Bus Architecture DCA + Aux Sub

Subgroup Glue & Monitor Routing

Group all Backing Vocals onto a dedicated DCA/Subgroup for instant -10 dB transitions during prayer and preaching, keep spoken-word channels 100% dry, and feed subwoofers strictly from the dedicated Aux Sub bus (Kick, Bass, Floor Tom, Keys 1).

4. Final Polish -1.0 dBTP Ceiling

Broadcast Mastering & Delivery

Apply linear-phase sub cleanup (HPF 28–32 Hz), gentle 1.5–2.5 dB SSL/VCA bus glue compression, mid/side stereo widening above 250 Hz (keeping bass 100% mono below 140 Hz), and True-Peak brickwall limiting calibrated for Spotify (-14 LUFS) and sermon archives (-16 LUFS).

PreSonus Capture 32×32 USB • Adobe Audition Post-Production

Recording & Editing Studio

End-to-end 24-bit multitrack stem capture via PreSonus Capture, surgical stem & sermon editing in Adobe Audition, and broadcast mastering for Dunamis Pod (Spotify & Telegram).

1. PreSonus Capture 24-Bit / 48 kHz USB

Live Multitrack Stem Capture

Tap all active console channels Pre-EQ / Pre-Fader over USB 2.0 with -18 dBFS average RMS and at least 6–12 dB peak headroom so drum transients never clip at 0.00 dBFS. Verify Ch 13 (LV) and Ch 17 (BV 4) show distinct waveforms during line check, engage Record Lock, and press M to drop live markers at Prayer, Worship, Choir, and Sermon start.

2. Virtual Soundcheck & Archival .capture + WAV Stems

Stem Transfer & Virtual Rehearsal

Use PreSonus Capture’s one-click Virtual Soundcheck (Digital Return mode on the console) to dial in Fat Channel gates, EQ, and monitor wedges before the band arrives. After service, save the .capture project and export discrete 24-bit .wav stems into YYYY-MM-DD_Service_Venue/Raw_Stems/ before opening any DAW.

3. Adobe Audition Prep Spectral + De-Bleed

Surgical Stem & Sermon Editing

Load stems into Adobe Audition Multitrack: run Diagnostics → DeClipper on any 0.00 dBFS drum/keys spikes (after -6 dB clip gain), use Adaptive Noise Reduction + DeHummer (50 Hz) on speech mics, run Diagnostics → Delete Silence (-38 dB) with 15 ms crossfades on idle Backing Vocal tracks, and nudge lagging drum/bass stems (+2.93 ms Tom 1, +4.04 ms Floor Tom, +1.79 ms Bass DI) into phase lock with Kick.

4. Broadcast Export Spotify + Telegram

Dunamis Pod Mixdown & Delivery

Trim non-sermon announcements with ripple-delete crossfades, level dynamic preaching swings with manual Clip Gain + 3.2:1 Tube-Modeled Compression, and brickwall-limit via Adobe Audition’s Hard Limiter (-16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP for sermons; -14 LUFS / -1.0 dBTP for full-band worship). Export 320 kbps CBR MP3 for Dunamis Pod on Spotify and 96 kbps MP3/M4A for fast mobile streaming on Telegram.

Emergency Mid-Service Recovery (If Singers Swap Mics Live)

Console Soft-Swap (5 Seconds)

Swap Console Digital Input Sources

Open HOME → Input → Digital Patching on the Console. Route Channel 17 (Lead Vocal) to read the swapped BV Port, and route that BV channel to Port 17.

Capsule Compensation EQ

Apply Vocal Rescue Curve

Lower HPF to 80 Hz, boost +4.5 dB @ 240 Hz (warmth), cut -4.5 dB @ 786 Hz (nasal honk), and add +3.5 dB shelf @ 8 kHz.

Monitor & DCA Check

Verify Wedge & Capture Routing

Ensure the swapped channel is pulled out of the BV Mute/DCA group so the Lead Vocalist is never muted between songs.
