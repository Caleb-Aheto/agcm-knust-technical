Welcome to the official hub of the Assemblies of God Campus Ministry (AGCM KNUST) Technical Department—the church’s dedicated audio engineering team powering live Front-of-House (FOH) mixing, 24-bit multitrack recording, surgical editing, mastering, and campus-wide sound setup.

General Audio Overview

Our Core Audio Responsibilities

Great church and live event sound starts from the ground up: clean stage cabling and gain staging (-18 dBFS), phase-aligned low end, subtractive EQ that carves space for every instrument, and clear vocal authority.
