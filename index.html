Audio Engineering & Sound Department Home

Welcome to the official site of the Assemblies of God Campus Ministry (AGCM KNUST) Technical Department. We are the church’s dedicated audio engineering team, focused on live Front-of-House (FOH) mixing, multitrack recording, audio editing, mastering, and equipment conveyance across KNUST.

General Audio Overview

Our Core Audio Responsibilities

Great church and live event sound starts from the ground up: clean stage cabling and gain staging (-18 dBFS), phase-aligned low end, subtractive EQ that carves space for every instrument, and clear vocal authority.
