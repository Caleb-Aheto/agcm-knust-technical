Core Audio Pillars

What We Do

01 • Live FOH 🎛️

1. Mixing

Front-of-House balance, -18 dBFS gain staging, stage monitor mixes, and surgical vocal clarity across campus venues.

02 • Broadcast 🎚️

2. Mastering

Broadcast loudness calibration (-14 LUFS music / -16 LUFS sermons) and -1.0 dBTP True-Peak limiting for Dunamis Pod.

03 • Multitrack 🎙️

3. Recording

24-bit / 48 kHz discrete multitrack USB stem capture in PreSonus Capture for worship ministrations and sermons.

04 • Post-Prod ✂️

4. Editing

Surgical stem de-bleed, spectral noise cleanup, drum phase alignment, and sermon prep in Adobe Audition.
